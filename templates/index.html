Foot Size Detection System | Built with Flask & OpenCV

About

This system uses computer vision to measure foot length and determine UK shoe size. Place a UK 1p coin (22mm diameter) next to the foot for scale reference.

Instructions:
  1. Select an image with a foot and a coin
  2. Click "Process Image"
  3. A window will open - click on HEEL first
  4. Then click on TOE
  5. Press any key to continue
  6. Results will be displayed automatically
